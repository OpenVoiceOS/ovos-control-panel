Voice profiles

Dashboard

The device can check that a wake word came from a voice it knows. Record a few clips of each person, give the profile a name, and the device keeps a voice fingerprint. This needs ovos-ww-verifier-plugin-speaker on the device.

What is stored
  • The recordings leave this browser, go to the device, and are deleted after the device reads them. Only a number fingerprint stays, and it stays on the device.
  • Record a person only with that person present and willing.
  • Enrolling and deleting are closed on the device by default. If the device answers that enrollment is off, a person with access to the device turns it on in the plugin configuration.

Enrolled voices

    Record a new voice

    Record three to five clips. Say a normal sentence each time, about three seconds, in the room the device sits in.

      Test a voice

      Record one fresh clip and ask the device whose voice it is. Nothing is saved: this only reports what the device hears.
