How the control panel itself looks and behaves: its name, colour and theme, where its recommendations come from, its stores, the largest file it takes, which added pages it shows, and the token that guards it.

Appearance

The name is shown in the header, the window title and on the sign in page. The colour is used for buttons, links and the chosen page in the menu.

A larger text helps people who see less well, so think twice before taking the button away.

    Recommendations

    Where the plugins recommended for a language come from, on Setup and on the Plugins page. A distribution can ship its own; where it has none for a language, OpenVoiceOS's own are used.

    Stores

    A store is a list of skills and plugins that someone has checked for a kind of device. Each store you add here is a choice under Show on the Skills and Plugins pages, beside the OVOS release and everything on PyPI. The panel comes with none.

      Uploads

      The largest file a page may put on the device, such as a film or a song on the Media page. A device that is never meant to hold films can take less.

      Added pages

      Pages that skills and plugins add to the panel. Untick one to take it out of the menu on this device.

          Access token

          Checking…

          A good token is long and random. Press the button to have a strong one made for you, write it down, then save. It is stored in your configuration and needed to sign in. This session stays signed in after you change it.

          Make one yourself instead

          For example, the output of python3 -c "import secrets; print(secrets.token_urlsafe(32))".
